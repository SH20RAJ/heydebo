## One final principle to build into the app

Instead of measuring **hours studied**, measure **missions completed**.

When you open the app, it shouldn't ask *"How long did you study?"*

It should ask:

> **"Did you complete today's mission?"**

That subtle shift encourages finishing meaningful work instead of accumulating hours, and it aligns much better with your goals of excelling academically, mastering DSA, and using teaching as a way to learn.
