# ⏱️ What to Do During Breaks

### 5–10 min

* Drink water
* Stretch
* Walk
* Check next classroom
* Review flashcards

### 20–30 min

* Revise previous lecture
* Solve 1 easy DSA problem
* Organize notes

### 45–90 min

* Go to library
* Solve 2 medium DSA problems
* Finish assignments
* Record a short concept explanation if you have a quiet place

---

# 📍 Library vs Hostel

### Go to Library

* Gap ≥ 60–90 min
* Need deep focus
* DSA
* Assignments
* Mock tests
* Recording scripts

### Stay in Hostel

* Need power nap
* Need fresh clothes
* Short gap
* Need recovery

* **Monday–Friday:** 1 short educational video/day (10–20 min recording).
